Indicators are estimates for situational awareness, not navigational or trading advice. "Pre-crisis norm" is a curated baseline of daily transit calls for the steady state just before the Feb 2026 Hormuz crisis (Hormuz ~94, Suez ~40, Bab el-Mandeb ~35, Malacca ~225, Cape ~90) from IMF PortWatch / Suez Canal Authority / EIA. For Suez, Bab el-Mandeb and the Cape this is the recent Houthi-era normal, so their % reflects the Hormuz crisis on top of the earlier Red Sea disruption. Transit counts are the latest reported COMPLETE day from IMF PortWatch (updated ~weekly, so the "as of" date typically lags several days); if the newest day is still provisional, the previous complete day is shown. AIS-derived counts may be affected by GPS jamming and dark activity.
